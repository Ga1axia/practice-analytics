## First sign-in (both)

1. Open the URL above. Choose **Microsoft**.
2. Confirm the header shows your name (Avery Cobe / Arnita Serri). If it does not, stop and tell Taihei — the account is not linked to time entries yet.
3. Wait a few seconds the first time. The app claims every job you have hours on so it appears under **My projects**.
4. Pages, left to right:
   - **Today** — overdue and due-this-week checklist items
   - **My tasks** — tasks across your jobs
   - **My calendar** — deadlines
   - **My projects** — your jobs. Sort by **recent hours** or **A–Z**; the choice is remembered.
   - **My timecard** — hours, efficiency, and phase mix from CORE

Shortcut: **Today (C)** in the header jumps to today’s calendar.

Hours, contracts, and project names come from CORE + the Project List. If a job is missing, log time in CORE (or ask to be added as a member) and refresh.

## Avery — project lead

You see everything Arnita sees, plus money and team tools on jobs where you are **Lead** (Project List manager, or assigned lead).

**My projects**
- Cards show **Lead** vs **Member**.
- Lead cards show **contract** and **outstanding**.
- Top KPIs include your **contract book** for lead jobs only.

**Open a job**
- **Contract / outstanding** in the header.
- **Team** — add or remove members so they can see the job even before they log hours.
- **Start schedule** if the checklist is empty — pick a kickoff date (Interior dates stay blank until you set them).
- **Tasks** — assign people, update status and dates. Task names are fixed; notes go in **Client’s comments**.
- **Meetings / Box links / messages** — client-facing comms on that job.
- **Your hours** still come from CORE, not from this screen.

If you are only a **Member** on a job (not lead), it behaves like Arnita’s view: no contract figures, you cannot edit the team.

**Today** for you is labeled **Project lead** and counts how many jobs you lead.

## Arnita — employee

**Today** is the home page: overdue work, due this week, then jump into tasks / calendar / timecard / projects.

**My projects**
- Every job you have hours on, plus any job a lead added you to.
- Sorted by **your last CORE timesheet date** (newest at the top) or **A–Z**. The toggle stays on your next visit.
- Cards show **Member** (or **Lead** if you manage that job). No contract / outstanding unless you are lead.

**Open a job**
- Your hours and billable hours on that job, hours by phase.
- Schedule, calendar, tasks (you can update items assigned to you).
- Team list is visible; you cannot add/remove people.

**My timecard**
- Billable hours and efficiency (firm target 85%).
- Click chart bars to drill into entries or open a project.
- To **change** hours, edit the entry in CORE; this page will catch up after the next sync.

## Shared habits

- Filter **Active** vs **All** on My projects, and **Recent hours** vs **A–Z**. Search by client, name, or job code (`26-040`).
- A job can sit on both of your lists. Avery leads it; Arnita is a member once she has hours or is added.
- Additional Services and Interior often run in parallel with base phases — they are separate cards/phases, not a replacement for CDs.
- Do not use `/demo` for real work. That path is the demo tour with fake accounts.

**If something looks wrong**
- Name missing after sign-in → profile `employee_name` not set.
- Job missing → no hours in CORE under your name, and you are not a member.
- Hours stale → CORE entry not synced yet; ask an admin to run BQE time sync.
- Cannot edit team / contract → you are a member, not lead, on that job.
